Aerial Photography & Drone Video in Tea, South Dakota

Lincoln County's Fastest-Growing City

Tea has quietly become one of the most active real estate markets in the Sioux Falls metro, and it earns the title of Lincoln County's fastest-growing city year after year. New subdivisions keep pushing south and west of town, turning open farmland into finished streets almost faster than the MLS can keep up. When a market moves this quickly, the listings that photograph best are the ones that sell first — and nothing frames a Tea home, its lot, and its place in a brand-new neighborhood like a well-flown aerial. Drone photography in Tea has gone from a nice extra to something agents lean on to stand out.

Because Tea sits just a few minutes down I-29 from Sioux Falls, we can offer genuine same-day scheduling here. There's no travel fee and no waiting days for an open slot. An agent who lists a home in the morning can often have edited drone photos and video in hand before the weekend showings begin.

New Construction & the Tea-Ellis Road Corridor

Much of Tea's growth has followed the Tea-Ellis Road corridor, where builders are converting fields into full subdivisions at a remarkable pace. New construction is one of the strongest use cases for aerial photography: a ground-level photo can't show a buyer how a fresh development connects to the interstate, the schools, and the wider Sioux Falls metro, but a drone flyover captures all of it in a single sweeping shot. For the builders and listing agents working the newest phases off Tea-Ellis Road, aerial video also doubles as marketing collateral for the next release of lots.

Tea's builders regularly need more than listing photography — they need aerial progress documentation too. A monthly flyover over an active build creates a visual record of the project and gives pre-sale buyers something compelling to watch as their home and their street take shape. We handle both the finished-listing shoot and the ongoing construction record from the same certified crew.

A Metro-Edge Market with Room to Grow

What makes Tea unusual is how close it sits to everything while still having room to expand. Buyers get a short commute into the Sioux Falls metro, the value of Lincoln County schools, and a small-town feel that photographs beautifully from the air. Aerial photography is the fastest way to communicate that combination — one overhead pass shows a buyer the proximity to the interstate, the surrounding open land, and how a new subdivision fits into the larger picture south of Sioux Falls.

Our pilots fly every Tea listing the same way: FAA Part 107 certified, insured, and briefed on the airspace before we ever leave the truck. That discipline matters this close to the Sioux Falls metro, where controlled airspace and traffic patterns demand a pilot who knows the rules cold.

Aerial photography and video signal to a seller that you take their home seriously — and to buyers scrolling the MLS, an overhead hero shot stops the thumb every time.
