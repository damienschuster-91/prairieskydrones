Page & Content
Template Guide

Use this every time you add or update content. These rules ensure SEO consistency, brand coherence, and rankings across all pages.

🏠 Homepage index.html

ElementRule
<title>[City] Drone Photography | [Service] | Prairie Sky Drones — max 60 chars
Meta DescriptionInclude city, service type, and one USP. 140–155 chars.
H1One per page. Must include primary keyword + city. Playfair Display style.
Hero Stats4 stats max. Update quarterly with real numbers when available.
Services Grid6 cards max. Each: service tag + H3 title + 2-line description + starting price.
Testimonials3 minimum. Must include agent name, brokerage, and SD city.
Coverage CitiesAlways list all covered cities. Update city-pill grid if coverage expands.
CTA BannerOne per page near bottom. Links to contact. Sky blue gradient.

👤 About Page about.html

ElementRule
Pilot CardSticky left column. Replace placeholder photo ASAP. Always show FAA badge.
CertificationsAlways list: FAA Part 107, $1M insurance, LAANC authorization.
Coverage TableUpdate when service area changes. City, county, and travel fee columns required.
ToneFirst-person plural ("we"). Local-specific details. No corporate boilerplate.
EquipmentList specific drone models. Update when equipment changes.

📰 Articles Index resources/

ElementRule
Featured ArticleAlways the newest or highest-traffic piece. Full-width card with image.
Article CardsThumb + category tag + date + H3 + 1-line excerpt + "Read →" link.
Sidebar CTASienna box, always first in sidebar. Update copy quarterly.
Frequency2 articles/month minimum for local SEO compounding effect.

📄 Single Article resources/[slug].html

ElementRule
<title>[Keyword] in [City], SD | Prairie Sky Drones
H1Include keyword and city. One per article. Max 70 chars.
Word count900–1,400 words. Under 800 rarely ranks for local intent.
City mentionsMention Sioux Falls (or target city) at least 3× naturally in body.
Internal links2–3 links to service pages or other articles. Never orphan an article.
CTAFull-width CTA banner at bottom of every article.
Back linkAlways include "← Back to Resources" at top.
