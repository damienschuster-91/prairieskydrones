A Fast-Growing Brandon Market That Rewards Great Aerials

Brandon has become one of the most active listing markets in Minnehaha County, and the pace shows no sign of slowing. New residential developments like Aspen Crossing keep adding rooftops every season, and the reputation of the Brandon Valley school district pulls a steady stream of buyers out of Sioux Falls and into town. When inventory moves this quickly, the listings that photograph best are the ones that sell first — and nothing frames a Brandon home, its lot, and its proximity to schools and parks like a well-flown aerial.

Because Brandon sits only a short drive east of Sioux Falls, we can offer genuine same-day scheduling here. There's no travel fee and no waiting days for an available slot. An agent who lists a home in the morning can often have edited drone photos and video in hand before the weekend showings begin.

New Construction Along the I-90 Corridor

Growth in Brandon has followed the I-90 corridor and the streets radiating out from Brandon Valley High School, where builders are turning fields into finished neighborhoods at a remarkable clip. New construction is one of the strongest use cases for aerial work: a ground-level photo can't show a buyer how a new subdivision connects to schools, trails, and the highway, but a drone flyover captures all of it in a single sweeping shot. For builders and listing agents working the Aspen Crossing area and the newer developments on Brandon's north and east edges, aerial video also doubles as marketing collateral for the next phase of lots.

We handle progress documentation as well as listing photography. For homes still under construction, a monthly flyover creates a visual record of the build and gives pre-sale buyers something compelling to watch as their home takes shape.

Acreage & Rural Properties East of Brandon

Head east of town toward the Split Rock Creek area and the listings change character entirely. Acreage properties, hobby farms, and rural homesites along Split Rock Creek are among our most common shoot requests in the Brandon market — and they're exactly the kind of property that suffers most from ground-only photography. Buyers considering an acreage want to understand the shape of the land, the tree lines, the outbuildings, and how the creek and terrain lay across the parcel. A drone answers all of those questions in the first ten seconds of a video tour.

Our pilots fly these rural parcels the same way we fly in-town listings: FAA Part 107 certified, insured, and briefed on the airspace before we ever leave the truck. That matters near the Sioux Falls metro, where controlled airspace and traffic patterns require a pilot who knows the rules cold.
