The Gateway to Devils Tower

Sundance sits at the doorstep of one of the most recognizable landmarks in the American West. As the closest town of any size to Devils Tower National Monument, this corner of Crook County trades on a landscape that photographs like almost nowhere else — rolling ponderosa foothills, the Bear Lodge Mountains, and that unmistakable stone monolith rising on the horizon. For anyone marketing property here, that setting is an asset, and drone photography in Sundance is how you put it to work. An aerial frame can place a listing in its full context: the ridgelines, the treed draws, and, when a property's sightlines allow, the distant profile of Devils Tower itself as a dramatic backdrop that no ground-level photo can capture.

It's worth being clear about the airspace. The monument and the land immediately around it carry flight restrictions, and our Part 107 pilots respect those boundaries without exception. We fly the property we're hired to shoot — legally, safely, and outside restricted zones — and we know exactly where those lines fall so a shoot near the Tower never puts a client in a difficult position.

Ranchland & Acreage in Crook County

The real estate market around Sundance is built on land. Crook County is ranch country — cattle operations, hay ground, recreational acreage, and rural homesites spread across parcels that are measured in quarter-sections rather than city lots. This is precisely the kind of property that ground photography fails to do justice. A buyer weighing a 160-acre spread wants to understand the fence lines, the water, the stands of timber, the outbuildings, and how the terrain rolls from one boundary to the next. Aerial photography answers all of it at once. A single sweeping drone pass shows the shape and scale of a place in a way a walk-through gallery simply cannot, which is why acreage and ranch listings are among the strongest use cases for drone work anywhere in the county.

We shoot these rural parcels the same way we approach every job: FAA Part 107 certified, fully insured, and briefed on the local airspace before we ever unfold a drone. For agents listing recreational land near Devils Tower or working ranch transactions across Crook County, aerial video also serves double duty as marketing that stands out in a market where the land is the whole story.
