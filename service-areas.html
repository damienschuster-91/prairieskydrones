Coverage · Two Pilot Hubs

Drone Photography Service Areas — South Dakota, Nebraska, Wyoming & Montana

Prairie Sky Drones covers all of South Dakota from two pilot hubs — Sioux Falls in the southeast and Rapid City in the west — plus border communities in Nebraska, Wyoming, and Montana. Explore our city service areas below.

Sioux Falls Region

Sioux Falls & Southeast South Dakota

Pilot Jax Schuster covers Minnehaha, Lincoln, and eight surrounding counties — with same-day scheduling and no travel fee within 40 miles of Sioux Falls.

Brandon · South Dakota

Fast-growing Minnehaha County community with new developments and acreage along Split Rock Creek.

Tea · South Dakota

Lincoln County's fastest-growing city, packed with new construction just south of Sioux Falls.

Harrisburg · South Dakota

Premium listings in one of South Dakota's top-ranked school districts along the I-29 corridor.

Dell Rapids · South Dakota

Historic quartzite downtown and scenic Big Sioux River acreage in northern Minnehaha County.

Canton · South Dakota

The Lincoln County seat, blending historic in-town homes with river-bottom farmland and acreage.
Rapid City & Black Hills Region

West South Dakota, the Black Hills & Border States

Our Rapid City hub covers Pennington, Meade, Lawrence, and the surrounding western counties, extending into border communities in Nebraska, Wyoming, and Montana.

Rapid City · South Dakota

The booming hub of West River SD, where every listing gets a dramatic Black Hills backdrop.

Spearfish · South Dakota

Northern gateway to the Black Hills, with canyon-adjacent estates and Spearfish Creek views.

Sturgis · South Dakota

Home of the world's largest motorcycle rally and a unique commercial and ranchland market.

Deadwood · South Dakota

A National Historic Landmark city of gulch, casino, and mountain properties in the Black Hills.

Hot Springs · South Dakota

Southern Black Hills town known for red sandstone architecture and Fall River County ranchland.

Chadron · Nebraska

The largest Nebraska Panhandle city, set against the dramatic Pine Ridge landscape.

Sundance · Wyoming

Crook County seat and gateway to Devils Tower, with wide-open Wyoming ranchland.

Gillette · Wyoming

Wyoming's energy capital, with industrial documentation and an energy-driven housing market.
